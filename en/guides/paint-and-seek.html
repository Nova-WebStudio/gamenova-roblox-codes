1. Beginner guide: how to play

Paint and Seek is a multiplayer hide-and-seek game by Blend In Or Die / Ikitai Studios. Each round, players split into two camps:

  • Hiders: during prep time, they paint themselves the color of the scenery to blend into walls, floors and objects. Their goal: survive until time runs out.
  • The seeker: must find all the hiders before the round ends.

The game is fast, clever and competitive: everything comes down to the quality of your camouflage (as a hider) and your ability to spot the odd one out in the scenery (as a seeker). The Coins you earn unlock traits, perks and skins.

💡 Quick answer: as a hider, sample the exact color of the surface you press against with the eyedropper, paint early, break up your outline in a cluttered corner, and move before you're spotted.

2. Hide like a pro (hider)

Good camouflage rests on three pillars: color, shape and spot.

The eyedropper on the RIGHT surface

This is the golden rule. Point the eyedropper directly at the surface you'll hide against, not a wall that "looks similar" across the room. Shades vary with lighting and texture: sampling the exact color of the right spot makes you nearly invisible. It's the #1 beginner mistake.

Break up your outline

The right color isn't enough: on a flat wall, you remain a recognizable human shape. Place yourself where your outline is hard to read — in a corner, against a cluttered object, mixed in with furniture. That way you become part of the scenery.

Timing

  • Paint early: use all of your prep time, before the seeker arrives. Getting caught mid-paint means getting spotted.
  • Reposition: change spots before your position becomes obvious. Staying still too long always gives you away eventually.

3. Seek effectively (seeker)

As a seeker, your goal is to flush out all hiders before time runs out. Here's how to be deadly:

  • Grab detection perks: they're the most useful for spotting well-camouflaged hiders and scanning faster (see Coins section).
  • Spot "almost-right" colors: a hider who sampled the wrong shade subtly stands out. Train your eye to catch those gaps.
  • Look for human outlines: even well-painted, a hider on a flat surface keeps a recognizable shape. Scan the contours.
  • Scan methodically: clear zones one by one rather than running around. Static hiders eventually fall.

4. Coins, traits & perks

Coins are the main currency of Paint and Seek. You earn them slowly in matches (or quickly with codes), and spend them on:

  • Traits (via gacha): bonuses unlocked randomly.
  • Perks: notably detection perks for the seeker — the most rewarding buy.
  • Legendary weapon skins for the seeker, character cosmetics, paint effects and prop skins.
💡 Priority: useful perks before cosmetics. Skins don't help you win — perks do. Keep the Paint and Seek codes to top up your Coin balance for free.

6. Maps & repositioning

Paint and Seek has no character tier list: it's the maps that vary in difficulty (some are easier for hiders, others harder for the seeker to scan). Knowing the maps gives you an edge on both sides:

  • As a hider: spot cluttered corners, objects that break up your outline, and surfaces with easy-to-match colors.
  • As a seeker: learn favorite hiding spots and blind angles to scan efficiently.
  • Repositioning: on open maps, change spots regularly; on dense maps, use the clutter to blend in.

7. Mistakes to avoid

  • Eyedropper on the wrong surface: sampling an "approximate" color gives you away instantly. Aim at the exact surface.
  • Painting too late: getting caught mid-paint = spotted. Paint early.
  • Staying still all round: a static hider always falls eventually. Reposition.
  • Sitting on a flat wall: even well-painted, your outline stands out. Pick a spot that breaks up your shape.
  • Spending everything on skins: prioritize useful perks, especially as a seeker.
  • Forgetting to join the group: without it, codes won't work.
