Paint and Seek tips (hider & seeker)

1

Eyedropper on the RIGHT surface

Point the eyedropper directly at the wall/object you'll hide against — not a similar wall across the room.

2

Paint before pressure

Use all of your prep time: paint early, before the seeker arrives, so you're not caught mid-action.

3

Break up your outline

Pick a spot where your outline is hard to read (corners, cluttered objects): color isn't enough, shape matters.

4

Rotate before you're spotted

Move before your position becomes obvious: staying still too long eventually gives you away.

5

Seeker: grab detection perks

As a seeker, detection perks are well worth it: they make scanning hiding spots far easier.

6

Survive to the end

As a hider, the goal is to last until the round ends: patience and repositioning beat static spots.

7

Spend Coins wisely

Prioritize useful perks over cosmetic skins if you want to win. Keep codes to top up your balance.

About Paint and Seek

Paint and Seek is a multiplayer hide-and-seek game on Roblox developed by the group Blend In Or Die (Ikitai Studios), released in April 2026 and already past 26 million visits. Each round, hiders get prep time to paint themselves the color of the scenery and blend into walls, floors and objects, while a seeker must find them all before time runs out.

The heart of the game is smart camouflage: using the eyedropper on the right surface, breaking up your outline, and rotating at the right moment. On the seeker side, detection perks make the difference. The game features traits (gacha), perks and weapon skins bought with Coins.

Paint and Seek codes hand out free Coins — the fastest way to unlock traits and perks. Real progress, though, comes from mastering camouflage and reading the game.
